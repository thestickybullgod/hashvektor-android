# Code of Conduct

## Standards

Participants must:

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Discuss ideas without harassment or personal attacks.
- Protect sensitive data and private security findings.
- Avoid spam, deceptive reviews, and unrelated promotion.
- Keep discussions relevant to HashVektor and its Android app.

Unacceptable behavior includes threats, harassment, discrimination, doxxing, sexualized
content, deliberate disruption, and sharing another person's private information.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide, or remove content and may restrict participation in the project
when behavior harms the community or conflicts with this policy.

Report serious conduct concerns through GitHub's reporting tools or the repository maintainers'
private channels.
