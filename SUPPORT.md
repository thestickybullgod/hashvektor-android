# Support

Thanks for using HashVektor for Android. Use the channel below that best matches what you
need.

## Ask a question

For installation, usage, supported algorithms, checksum formats, or Android compatibility,
start a thread in [GitHub Discussions Q&A](https://github.com/thestickybullgod/hashvektor-android/discussions/categories/q-a).

## Report a bug

[Open a bug report](https://github.com/thestickybullgod/hashvektor-android/issues/new?template=bug_report.yml).
Include:

- HashVektor version
- Android version and device model
- Steps to reproduce the issue
- What you expected and what happened
- A sanitized screenshot or log, if useful

Please check existing issues first. Never attach private files, personal data, passwords,
signing keys, or purchase receipts.

## Suggest an improvement

Share feature requests in [GitHub Discussions Ideas](https://github.com/thestickybullgod/hashvektor-android/discussions/categories/ideas).
Describe the problem you are trying to solve and any privacy or permission implications.

## Google Play purchase

HashVektor Pro is a lifetime activation purchased through Google Play. To restore an existing
purchase, open HashVektor's purchase controls and choose **Restore**. If the purchase still
doesn't appear, check that Google Play is signed in to the account that made the purchase and
that Play can reach its services.

For account-specific payment issues, use Google Play's purchase support. Do not post order
numbers, receipts, or account details in a public issue or Discussion.

## Security and privacy

Do not report a suspected vulnerability in a public issue or Discussion. Follow the private
reporting instructions in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). For privacy questions, see
[Security and privacy](docs/security-privacy.md).

## Response expectations

HashVektor is maintained by a small team. We review reports and questions as time allows, but
cannot guarantee a response time or that every feature suggestion will be implemented.
